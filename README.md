# ⚽ Football Talent: Scout & Career v0.5

> **Scout. Develop. Transfer. Build your football empire. 🌍🏆**

Football Talent is a football management game where **you run your own scouting agency**. Find promising players, analyze their potential, develop them, negotiate transfers and grow from a small agency into a global football empire.

## 🎮 What can you do?

- 🔎 **Scouting** — search for young talents around the world.
- 📊 **Analyzer** — study rating, potential, form and player stats.
- 👥 **Players** — manage your discovered footballers.
- 🏋️ **Training** — improve players and increase their value.
- 💰 **Transfers** — buy and sell players and manage your budget.
- 🤝 **Clubs** — work with clubs and follow the transfer market.
- 🏟️ **Matches** — simulate football matches and follow results.
- 📅 **Career** — level up your agency and unlock new opportunities.
- 💶 **Finance** — keep control of your money and investments.
- 🏅 **Achievements** — complete goals and build your reputation.
- ⚙️ **Settings** — customize your game experience.

## 🌟 Real Players + Fictional Talents

The game includes a small reference database of real footballers alongside fictional generated talents.

Fictional players can have different ratings, potential, ages, positions and values — so every scouting career can develop differently.

> **Note:** Real-player information in this prototype is for game/reference purposes and may not represent the latest real-world values.

## 📈 Build your agency

Start with your budget and reputation, discover promising players, make smart decisions and gradually expand your agency.

Your goal:

**Small agency → Professional scout → Big agency → Global company → Football Empire 👑**

## 💡 Why play?

You are not controlling just one footballer.

**You control the people who discover the next stars.**

One great scouting decision can turn an unknown teenager into a world-class player — and transform your agency at the same time.

## 🛠️ How to run

### Option 1 — Browser

1. Download the project.
2. Extract the ZIP archive.
3. Open `index.html` in a browser.
4. Start your scouting career.

### Option 2 — GitHub Pages

1. Create a GitHub repository.
2. Upload `index.html` to the repository root.
3. Open **Settings → Pages**.
4. Select deployment from the `main` branch.
5. Open your published GitHub Pages address.

> For the basic web version, `index.html` is the essential game file. `README.md` is only the project description/documentation.

## 💾 Save system

Your game progress is stored in the browser using **localStorage**.

If you clear the browser's site data, your local save may be lost. A future version can add cloud saves and account synchronization.
